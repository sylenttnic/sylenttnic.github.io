Trust Built the Slow Way

  2. 02
  3. 03 Started With a Truck and a Chainsaw. In 1987, after climbing timber for a logging crew, Ray Tolliver started the business with an old Ford pickup and a single chainsaw. What Happens When You Call

  1. Step 01: You Call, We Look

    Tell us what's going on: a leaning tree, storm damage, a stump that's been in the way for years. We'll walk through what a job like yours involves and get you a quote.

  2. Step 02: Access, Rigging, Safety

    On site, the crew works out how to reach the tree safely, rigging lines and clearing a path for anything coming down near your house, your fence, or the power line.

  3. Step 03: Cleanup and Haul-Away

    Branches get chipped, the wood gets stacked or hauled, and the yard gets left the way you'd want to find it.
