What We Do

Landscaping, Lawn Care, and Everything Between

Nine jobs on one crew's list, from mowing to tree removal.

  • Landscaping: design and installation, from bare ground to a finished yard.
  • Flagstone Walkways: a nonslip, textured path that lets water through instead of letting it pool.
  • Lawncare Maintenance: mowing, edging, and keeping the yard cut.
  • Privacy Fence: for the property line that needs one.
  • Borders: brick, stone, or other materials laid to keep grass out of your beds and hold the mulch and rain in.
  • Landscape Maintenance: the year-round upkeep that keeps a finished yard finished.
  • Outdoor & Landscape Lighting: low-voltage lighting, installed and maintained, for safety and curb appeal.
  • Pruning: shaping shrubs and trees by hand.
  • Tree Removal, Stump Grinding & Core Aeration, the heavy work: trees down, stumps out, lawns aerated.
