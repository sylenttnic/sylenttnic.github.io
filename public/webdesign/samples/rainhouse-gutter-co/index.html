Four things we do, every week

01

New Gutter Installation

Rainhouse Gutter Co. installs 5-inch and 6-inch K-style seamless aluminum gutters, sized to the roof in front of them. Larger roofs, or systems that need to be done with fewer downspouts, get an oversized downspout instead.

Most gutter companies call their work “seamless.” Here’s what that actually means: instead of joining short, factory-cut sections end to end, a seamless run is formed on site to the exact length of your roofline, so the only joints are at the corners and the downspout outlets.

Seamless aluminum gutters can last up to 20 years or more.

02

Gutter Cleaning

Debris and standing water work against a gutter system fast in this climate. Rainhouse Gutter Co. recommends a cleaning at least twice a year to keep water moving off the roof and away from the foundation.

03

Gutter Repair

Sagging sections, loose hangers, and small leaks: minor repairs to an existing system are handled by Rainhouse Gutter Co.’s own experienced installers.

04

Gutter Screens & Moss Control

Choose from over 20 screen colors to keep leaves and needles out of the system, and ask about an environmentally safe product to control moss on the roof above it, with a follow-up treatment recommended as needed.
