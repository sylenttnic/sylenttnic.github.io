What Happens When You Call

What happens when you call

  1. 01

    You call. Describe what's going on and get a straightforward answer about what it'll take.

  2. 02

    Permit, if the job needs one. Panel upgrades, generator installs and new circuits generally need a permit from the City of Rockford before work starts.

  3. 03

    Utility disconnect, if the job needs one. Some panel work needs the utility to pull the meter first, so nobody's working a live panel.

  4. 04

    The work. The repair, install or rewire happens, on the schedule you agreed to.

  5. 05

    Inspection and power-on. Permitted work gets inspected, then the power goes back on.

Good to Know

Questions Rockford asks

Will I need a permit for my electrical work?

In Rockford, panel upgrades, generator installs and new circuits generally need a permit from the city. Ask when you call and we'll tell you what your specific job needs.

Are you licensed and insured?

From what we can find, Illinois doesn't issue a state electrical licence, and Rockford's own contractor registration has no public way for us to verify it against. What we can tell you: Dale Merrow has run this shop under his own name since 1998, and all work is guaranteed and warrantied.

Will the work meet Rockford's electrical codes?

Any permitted job gets inspected before the power goes back on, which is what keeps it code-compliant. See "What happens when you call" above for how that works.

Is your work guaranteed?

Yes. All work is guaranteed and warrantied.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Estimates are free, whether it's a single outlet or a full rewire.

How do you accept payment?

Cash or check. We don't currently take credit cards.

How do I know if I need an electrician?

Flickering lights, warm outlets, breakers that trip often or a burning smell are all signs to stop and call an electrician rather than wait.
