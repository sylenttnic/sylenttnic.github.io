Questions people ask us.

What does the yard stock, and can I pick it up myself?

Yes. The yard stocks rock, mulch, dirt and soil, and gravel and sand. Swing by and load your own truck or trailer, or have it delivered.

Do you deliver, or do I need to come pick it up?

Either one. Come to the yard and load it yourself, or have your rock, mulch, dirt or gravel brought to you.

Do you charge for a quote?

No. Quotes and estimates are free, just ask.

What hardscape work do you build?

Patios, walkways and pathways, retaining walls with drainage, fire pits, fireplaces and fire tables, and water features from fountains to ponds.

Do you work outside Mankato?

Yes. We work in Mankato, North Mankato, Eagle Lake, Saint Peter, Lake Crystal, Madison Lake, Janesville, Waseca, Nicollet, Good Thunder, Le Center, St. Clair, and the surrounding area in southern Minnesota.
