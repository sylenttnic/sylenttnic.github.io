What we do

The lawn program

A program to give your lawn the total annual care it needs each year for control of weeds and insects, with fertilizer especially blended for this area.

  • Grub controls and fungicides, for an additional charge
  • Lawn aerification annually, at an additional charge

Tree and shrub care

Tree spraying and tree fertilizing for residential and commercial customers.

  • Tree & shrub insect control
  • Dutch elm disease control
  • Tree injections
  • Tree pruning, removal
  • Tree & shrub fertilizing
